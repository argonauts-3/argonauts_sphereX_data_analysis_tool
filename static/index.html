How to use the explorer

Pick a target

Use the curated drop-down, type a name resolved by Sesame, or enter J2000 RA/Dec in degrees. The name and coordinate fields are always editable.

Change settings, then press Show

The target, field of view, dates, detectors and alignment do not rebuild the images while you edit them. When anything changes, press Show (or Enter in a field) to apply the new settings. Playback controls act immediately.

Field of view

0.1–1.0 degrees, always rendered at 6.16″/pixel. Bigger fields download more archive data; cached cutouts make repeat visits fast.

Movie and A/B

Space plays or pauses; arrow keys step. Frames are strictly chronological by observation time, so same-epoch detectors sit next to each other. A/B differences are allowed only within one detector.

Display interpolation

Nearest keeps hard pixel edges; bilinear is smooth; bicubic and Lanczos are sharper and can ring around bright stars; Gaussian is soft. This is display-only and never changes flux, S/N, flags or the A/B arithmetic.

Preview and cancel

A new target first shows a small preview from one detector while the rest downloads. The banner says exactly what is shown. Cancel stops the build; downloaded cutouts are kept and reused next time.

Sharing

The URL contains the target, field, dates, detectors, frame, fps, display kernel and playback state. Add embed=1 to hide the form and side panels.

Colourbar and levels

The bar under the image gives the scale in MJy/sr above each frame's own median sky, for the detector on screen. The black and white levels (percent of that scale) re-window the image instantly. They cannot reveal pixels already clipped at the 1st and 99.5th percentile, and raising the black level also turns faint pixels black, so black then no longer means only “no data”. Difference images keep their own stretch.

Frames: tick boxes and wavelength window

Untick frames, drop flagged, faint or blended ones, or narrow the wavelength window. Hidden frames are skipped by Play, the frame slider and the A/B lists, and appear hollow in the plots. Clicking a hidden frame still shows it. These choices are saved in the URL (wl0, wl1, skip, lo, hi).

Measurements

The spectrum plots aperture flux against wavelength; the light curve plots it against date. SPHEREx samples a different wavelength on each visit, so compare epochs only inside a narrow wavelength window. Click a point to open its frame. The white ring is the frame on screen.
